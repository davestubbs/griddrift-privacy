# Privacy Policy

**Griddrift**
Last updated: June 2026

## Overview

Griddrift is a puzzle game developed and published by David Stubbs This policy explains what data is collected when you use the app and how it is handled.

The short version: Griddrift does not collect, store, or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no advertising.

## Data We Collect

Griddrift does not directly collect any personal information.

### In-App Purchases

If you make a purchase within Griddrift, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. Apple handles all payment information. Griddrift receives only confirmation that a purchase was made — no payment details, no name, no email address.

Apple's privacy policy applies to all App Store transactions and can be found at [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy).

### Crash Reporting

If the app crashes, Apple may collect anonymous diagnostic information as part of iOS. This is governed by your device's privacy settings and Apple's own data practices. Griddrift does not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting tools.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- No name, email address, or contact information
- No location data
- No device identifiers
- No usage analytics
- No advertising identifiers
- No data is sold or shared with third parties

## Children's Privacy

Griddrift does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. As no personal data is collected from any user, the app is safe for all ages.

## Your Puzzle Progress

Your solved puzzles and progress are stored locally on your device only, using iOS standard storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
